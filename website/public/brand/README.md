# Official brand assets

Downloaded on 2026-10-10. SVG files are stored unchanged; the website only
scales them proportionally. These third-party brand assets are governed by
their owners' brand guidelines, rather than Mample's source-code license.

## Google Play

- Files: `GetItOnGooglePlay_Badge_Web_color_English.svg` and
  `GetItOnGooglePlay_Badge_Web_color_Turkish.svg`.
- Source: [Google Play lockups, icons and badges](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/),
  localized badges > Digital > SVG.
- Render at 60px high with 15px clear space on every side, exceeding the
  28px minimum digital height and meeting the quarter-height clear-space rule.
- The download link remains inactive until a real store listing is configured
  in `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_PLAY_URL`.

## GitHub

- File: `GitHub_Lockup_Black_Clearspace.svg`.
- Source: [GitHub logo guidelines](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo),
  [official logo package](https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip).
- The official combined symbol and wordmark, including its built-in clear
  space, is displayed on a white button linking to Mample's GitHub repository.
- This is a project link; it does not represent a co-branding partnership.
